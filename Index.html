Handled Projects

Clean Water Access

Providing rural communities with access to safe water and essential hygiene training.

We construct boreholes and solar-powered water pumps. By reducing the distance women and children walk for water, we improve health and school attendance rates.

Agricultural Training

Empowering youth with modern farming techniques to ensure food security and income.

Our workshops cover sustainable irrigation, crop rotation, and market access, transforming subsistence farming into profitable small businesses for youth.

Maternal Health

Improving healthcare services for mothers and infants in underserved regions.

We provide prenatal care kits, transport vouchers for emergencies, and community health education to ensure safer deliveries and healthier starts for newborns.
